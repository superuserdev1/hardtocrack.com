HardToCrack policy

We select for security, not generations.

HardToCrack does not add a device simply because it is the newest. We offer it only when it meets our standards for official GrapheneOS compatibility, memory protection, security architecture, long-term support, stability and value relative to real needs.

Our catalogue can evolve as new manufacturers and devices meet those standards. This allows us to add future compatible platforms without tying the HardToCrack brand to Google Pixel.

Why don’t we currently offer Pixel 11?The Pixel 11 series is not part of our selection while it falls short of the memory protection and hardware security standards we require. GrapheneOS has specifically warned that this generation lacks ARM MTE support and currently recommends Pixel 8, 9 and 10 over Pixel 11. If the technical conditions change, HardToCrack will review its decision based on technical evidence, not product marketing.
